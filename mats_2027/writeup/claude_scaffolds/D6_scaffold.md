## D6. The AV imports famous names it knows, rather than misreading the ones in front of it

### Beat 1 — the observation (cricket)
Reading the explanations for Rahul Dravid's Wikipedia biography, I noticed the AV kept writing
about Don Bradman. Bradman is in that passage exactly once, and not as its subject: Dravid was
the first non-Australian to deliver the *Bradman Oration*. The records in the passage — most
balls faced in Tests, longest time spent batting — are Dravid's. The AV attached them to Bradman
instead, 21 claims across four token positions, including the flat assertion "The text is about
Don Bradman" and, twice, "Indian batsman Don Bradman".

But Bradman is not the interesting part, and it took counting to see that. Across the claims on
that passage the AV names Dravid 27 times, Bradman 21, **Tendulkar 20**, Lara 6, Richards 3,
Pietersen 2, Gavaskar 2, Hobbs 1. Tendulkar does not appear in the passage at all. Neither do
Lara, Richards, Pietersen, Gavaskar or Hobbs. Bradman only looked special because he is the one
imported name that happens to also be in the text — so he alone fit a story about misreading
what was there. The rest are simply famous batsmen the model knows, written onto a passage that
never mentions them.
