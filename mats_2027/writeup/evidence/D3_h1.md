# Evidence for D3 — H1 killed, with power
Source: H1-01 (pipeline/h1_probe.py), REL-01, hypotheses.md

## H1 as stated (2026-08-19)
Related-false claims are two populations under one label: some are faithful readouts the text
judge marks false ("activation-true, text-false"), the rest genuine confabulations. Predicted
signature: **bimodal** per-claim Δ for related-false claims, mixture fraction p ≈ 26–42%.
Kill condition, written in advance: unimodal → H1 dead.

## The cell
- 995 false claims → 975 RELATED (98.0%), 20 UNRELATED (REL-01). H1's cell = 975.
- Consequence: the paper's related-vs-unrelated Δ contrast is NOT reproducible on this corpus.

## Table D3.1 — the test, K=1, n=975
| detector | value | reading |

Internal control: TRUE claims, which H1 says nothing about, score ΔBIC **+2467** — higher still.

## Table D3.2 — why ΔBIC is disqualified: a single skewed hump, matched to the real data
| null | ΔBIC>10 false-positive rate | dip p<0.05 false-positive |
|---|---:|---:|
| gamma, skew matched to real (2.63), n=975, 200 draws | **200/200 = 100%** | 0/200 |
| median ΔBIC on that null | +846.5 (real data: +843.4 — BELOW the null median) | |

Re-run at real skew for K = 1, 4, 8, 16: ΔBIC>10 false-positives 100/100/100/99%. Unsalvageable.
The pre-registered rule had been validated against a null with skew ≈ 1.0; reality is 2.63
(related-false) and 5.63 (true). **The rule was revised after seeing the data → H1 verdict is
EXPLORATORY, not confirmatory.** Say this plainly.

Across H1's predicted 26–42%: **86–100% power**. Found nothing. **Bound: a mixture below ~20%
would have been missed.** H1 is dead in its stated form.

(Re-run at 400 draws on 30 Aug; the earlier 80-draw run gave 76% at p=0.26 — same quantity, ±9pp
sampling error. Quote the 400-draw numbers.)

## What a dead H1 does NOT show
That the AR treats these claims alike — only that their measured Δ does not separate. H3
(redundancy) would produce this null whether or not a mixture exists.
