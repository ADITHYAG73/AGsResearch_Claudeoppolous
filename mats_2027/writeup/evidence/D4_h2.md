# Evidence for D4 — H2: mechanism verified, payoff underpowered
Source: NOISE-03, H2-01 (pipeline/noise_analysis3.py), MATCH-02

## H2 as stated
The per-claim signal is noise-limited, not absent: averaging Δ over K resamples raises AUC.
Model: Δ_observed = Δ_underlying + ε. Kill condition (2026-08-19): variance does not fall as
~1/√K ⇒ noise is systematic ⇒ dead.

## The mis-specified kill condition (say this)
Observed log-log slope −0.273, not −0.50. Read literally, a partial kill. But the spread of claim
MEANS can never fall to zero — it converges on the real between-claim variation, which is the thing
we WANT to exist. Slope −0.5 only holds when noise swamps signal. The condition ignored the floor.

## Also corrected: the earlier noise ratios
NOISE-01's 1.41× and NOISE-02's 0.12× used the MEDIAN within-group sd — wrong statistic for a
variance decomposition (within-group sd is heavily skewed: median 0.00041, p90 0.00347). Do not quote
them. RMS-based: 0.5–1.4× depending on subset.
