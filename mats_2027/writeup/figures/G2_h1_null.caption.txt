If related-false claims were two populations — faithful readouts the text judge mislabels, plus genuine confabulations — their Δ would be bimodal. It is a single right-skewed hump (dip p = 0.992). The ΔBIC rule that was pre-registered DID fire (+843), but a single skewed hump matched to this data fires it in 200/200 draws, so it is disqualified; the dip test, which has the power shown in G3, is the detector that counts. True claims (gray) are shown for scale: the same shape, shifted right.
