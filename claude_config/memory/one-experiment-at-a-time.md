Adithya has asked to take up one experiment at a time and finish it to completion before
opening the next one. He explicitly asked Claude to **course-correct him when he starts
rearing away from the current experiment** — this is a standing instruction, not a one-off.

**Why:** his own `CLAUDE.md` §8 records a documented pattern of starting strong and not
finishing, and §8a diagnoses the cause as scoping rather than discipline. Interesting
tangents (and Claude-proposed detours) are the failure mode, not laziness.

**How to apply:** before proposing work, say which experiment it belongs to. If it belongs
to none, name it as a detour out loud and let him decide — do not lead with it. Give every
piece of work a completion condition up front. When he raises a new idea mid-experiment,
acknowledge it, write it into `mats_2027/experiments.md` or `hypotheses.md` as a parked
item, and return to the open experiment. See [[claude-drives-runpod-pods]].
