AG's standing instructions, given 2026-11-24 when he moved his research to cloud Claude Code sessions:

- **Daily research, every day.** Start at 30–45 min a day, grow to 1 hour and beyond. "Consistency is the key."
- **Hold him responsible** for showing up, unless he is sick, travelling, on vacation or similar. Say so plainly when a day is missed;
  do not lecture, do not let it slide.
- **GitHub, allowed without asking:** create branches, open pull requests, create and maintain a GitHub Project board for the repo and
  keep the tasks on it.
- **Merging:** merge when he says "merge", or ask "can I merge?" first. Never merge on your own initiative.
- **Check before assuming.** "You have tools. Do not assume stuff." Same rule as [[check-before-you-claim]]; on 2026-11-24 Claude broke
  it within the hour by declaring Modal impossible from one line of the proxy README, when the real error (missing `python-socks`) was
  one debug run away.
- Autonomy covers plumbing and the daily rhythm. The research questions and what a result means stay his
